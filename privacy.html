Privacy Policy for Sholly PDF

Effective date: September 11, 2026

Sholly PDF is a desktop PDF editor provided by Horeb Digital Network. This policy explains how Sholly PDF handles information when you use the application.

PDF files and editing content

Sholly PDF processes the PDF documents, annotations, images, and other content that you choose to open or create on your device. This content is processed locally by the application. Sholly PDF does not upload your documents or editing content to Horeb Digital Network.

Licensing and purchases

If you purchase Sholly PDF or activate a license, you interact with Gumroad. The application may send the license key you enter to Gumroad to verify that license. Payment information is collected and handled by Gumroad, not by Sholly PDF. Please review Gumroad's Privacy Policy for information about its practices.

Information we do not collect

Sholly PDF does not use advertising networks or third-party analytics to track your use of the app. We do not sell your personal information.

Website contact form

If you contact Horeb Digital Network through this website, we receive the information you choose to provide, such as your name, email address, and message, so we can respond to you. Please do not include sensitive information in a contact message.

Security and retention

Your local documents remain under your control on your device. License information is stored locally by the app to support activation and trial status. We use reasonable measures to protect information received through the website, but no internet transmission or storage method is completely secure.

Children's privacy

Sholly PDF is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when our practices or the application change. We will post the updated version on this page and revise the effective date.

Contact

For privacy questions, please contact Horeb Digital Network through the website contact form.
